How to use Mjolnir

1 Downloading files

Use this for any file that has a web link: a program, a video file, a document, a picture, a zip.

  1. Copy the link of the file.
  2. Click + Add URL at the top left. If you just copied a link, it is already pasted in. If not, paste it in the box.
  3. Mjolnir shows the file's name and size. To save somewhere else, click Browse. Otherwise it goes into your Downloads folder, in a folder called Mjolnir.
  4. Click Download. The file shows up in the list, with a bar that fills as it downloads.

Good to know

  • Pause and Resume. Click a download, then press Pause or Resume at the top. A paused download carries on from where it stopped, even after you close Mjolnir or switch off the computer.
  • Many files at once. Click Add Batch and paste one link on each line.
  • More choices. Right-click a download to open the file, show it in its folder, copy its link or remove it.
  • Finding things. The list on the left shows only the unfinished, finished or failed downloads, or one kind of file such as Video or Documents. The search box at the top right finds a download by its name.
  • If a download fails, Mjolnir tries again a few times by itself. If it still stops, click it and press Resume.
  • Closing the window does not stop your downloads. Mjolnir keeps working from the small icon near the clock. To close it completely, right-click that icon and choose Quit.

2 YouTube and Screener

These two have their own pages. You find them in the left list under Downloaders. If you do not see them, open Settings (the gear at the top right) and tick them under Downloaders.

YouTube

  1. Click YouTube in the left list.
  2. The first time only, click Download engine. It is a small helper (~18 MB) and it downloads just once.
  3. Paste the video link and click Get video.
  4. Choose Video or Audio only. For a video, pick the quality and the type. MP4 plays everywhere. MKV keeps the best quality.
  5. Click Download. You can watch the progress on the same page. The video is saved in the YouTube folder.

A link to a whole playlist shows a list of videos with tick boxes. Tick the ones you want and click Download.

Screener

  1. Click Screener in the left list.
  2. Type the company's short name, for example AVANTEL, or paste its screener.in link. Then click Find.
  3. Mjolnir shows how many annual reports, credit ratings and earnings call (concall) papers it found. Click Download.
  4. The files are saved in the Screener folder, with one folder for each company.

Good to know

  • To open their folders quickly, click YouTube or Screener under Categories in the left list.
  • For many at once, click Add Batch, pick the YouTube or Screener tab, and put one video link or company on each line. Playlists are not allowed in a batch.

3 The browser extension

The extension connects Chrome or Edge to Mjolnir. Your browser's downloads then come to Mjolnir, and you can send YouTube videos and company pages with one click. Mjolnir must be running for it to work.

Setting it up, once

  1. Click the puzzle icon at the top of Mjolnir. It lists the steps and has a Show me the folder button.
  2. In your browser, open the extensions page. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar (edge://extensions in Edge) and press Enter.
  3. Switch on Developer mode, then click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder.
  4. Click the Mjolnir icon in your browser's toolbar. It says Connected when everything works. If you cannot see the icon, click the puzzle piece in your browser and pin Mjolnir.

Using it

  • Normal downloads. When you download a file in your browser, a small Mjolnir window opens. Check the name and the folder, then click Start download.
  • YouTube and Screener. On a YouTube video or a screener.in company page, click the Mjolnir icon in the toolbar, then Download from this page.
  • Right-click. Right-click a link, a picture or a video and choose Download with Mjolnir.
  • Settings. The puzzle icon in Mjolnir lets you switch off the automatic catching of browser downloads, or leave small files to the browser.

4 Tools

Tools are small helpers inside Mjolnir. For now there are two.

Audio extractor

It takes the sound out of a video and saves it as an audio file. It does not change the sound in any way, so the quality stays the same, and it finishes in seconds.

  1. Click Audio extractor under Tools in the left list.
  2. Drop your video files on the page, or click Choose files.
  3. If a video has more than one sound track, for example two languages, tick the ones you want.
  4. Choose where to save: next to the video, or in a folder you pick.
  5. Click Extract audio. The finished files are listed under Results.

Clipboard

It remembers the last 25 things you copied, text and pictures, so you can use them again. It keeps working in the background while Mjolnir is open.

  1. Copy things as you normally do, with Ctrl+C or Cut. Nothing else to do.
  2. Click Clipboard under Tools in the left list to see them, newest first.
  3. Click an item, or its Copy button, to put it back on your clipboard. Then paste it anywhere. The item you used moves to the top.
  4. When the list is full, the item you used longest ago is removed to make room. Pictures can be PNG, JPG, GIF and more. Items bigger than 10 MB are skipped.

Use Pause before you copy something private, like a password. Copies from password managers are never kept.

Browser extension

Add the extension to Chrome or Edge once, and your browser's downloads can come to Mjolnir.
  1. In your browser, open the extensions page. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar (edge://extensions in Edge) and press Enter.
  2. Switch on Developer mode. It is a switch near the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked, then pick the Mjolnir extension folder.
  4. Click the Mjolnir icon in your browser's toolbar. It says Connected when everything works.
These two settings only apply to downloads your browser starts by itself. The Mjolnir icon in the toolbar and the right-click menu always work.

Send links from your phone

Turn this on, scan a code with your phone camera, and paste any link on your phone. It starts downloading here. YouTube and screener.in links go to their own downloaders. Windows may ask once to allow Mjolnir on private networks. Choose Allow.
